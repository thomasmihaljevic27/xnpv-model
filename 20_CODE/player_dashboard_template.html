What makes up this xNPV

Contract seasons: present value = (survival × projected value − cap hit) × discount, with discount = 1.03−k and survival 1 in the valuation season. RFA control rows come from rfa_terminal_value.py: projected value against the qualifying-offer cost, carried by the qualify-rate chain shown in the survival column (goalies: no qualify gates, survival 1), then discounted.

How to read this

The model re-values once per season. The page for a season reads only seasons that finished before it: the two prior seasons of WAR for skaters, the 50/30/20 blend of the three prior seasons for goalies. All of those are complete by July 1, so a page is in force from July 1 of its season to June 30 of the next. Picking a date picks the page in force that day. Production in the current season is not used until the following July, so a February trade is valued on the page built before that season began. Mid-season proration is not built yet.

Why a signing date can show the old contract. An extension signed during a season does not start until the next one. On the signing date, the page in force still values the contract being played out, including its RFA control years where they apply. The new contract's first page reads the season in which it was signed, so that page knows more than the team did at signing. The contract panel flags these cases.

What the line is and is not. The pages come from the validation panel (contract_npv_panel.csv). Every page is an ex-ante valuation. The sequence shows how the model's view of a player updates as production arrives. It is a diagnostic, not a score for historical trades.

Not shown. Draft-pick and non-roster-prospect values are not priced here. Players without a priced page from 2018-19 to 2025-26 are not listed. Skater WAR bars come from WAR_with_age.csv, with team splits summed. Goalie WAR comes from Goalies_WAR.csv, matched by cleaned name. The WAR chart is context; the projection's own inputs are in the breakdown table.
