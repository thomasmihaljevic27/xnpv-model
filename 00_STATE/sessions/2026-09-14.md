# Session 2026-09-14 - Whole-chain sweep to reduce the level tilt

- [CONTEXT] Thomas gave the go-ahead to experiment on the whole pipeline to reduce error in pricing, production projection and NPV at every level of player quality, including ideas raised earlier and not followed up. Follows the 09-13b findings (level tilt; fixed 2009-17 pull-back overshoots stars; market line refit does nothing).
- [DONE] `20_CODE/pipeline_experiment.py` v1.0: 19 variants through the production NPV engine, patched in-process, hashes checked. Every calibration rolling (fitted on 2009..t0-1). Starting-point rules L, L6, P, LB, LB3, RG, C (curve pass-through), M (market-informed, diagnostic); top-50 comparables; contracted-population hazard H; valuation-season hazard S0; combinations. Scored on the 4,677 played contract seasons. ~11 min.
- [DONE] `20_CODE/market_line_experiment.py` v1.0: 13 price-line specifications, rolling out of sample on 1,769 signings 2020-25, production censored estimator generalised. 15 s.
- [DONE] `20_CODE/games_line_experiment.py` v1.0: a games-aware value line (wins + games share) through the full chain with a rolling games projection, scored on its own currency. 2.5 min.
- [FINDING] The starting point is the lever. On 2020-25 pages (the fair test; the 2018-19 pages share the earlier fixed calibration window) rolling L takes bias from +3.5% to -2.9% overall and from +24% to -4% at 3+, cuts WAR error 5.7% and dollar error 2.8%, and removes the tilt (+$0.46M/WAR to -$0.01M). The variants of the rule land within 1-2 points of L. The earlier star overshoot came from the 2018-19 pages, where production was unbiased for stars and the pull-back ran 23% low; on 2020-25 pages production ran 24% high.
- [FINDING] The exit hazard is estimated on the wrong population: 10.6%/yr on all GP>=10 seasons vs 5.3% among players holding a contract for t+1; the chain prices only the latter. H alone is slightly worse on error (it had been offsetting over-projection); L+H is the best-calibrated combination (bias -0.5% overall, within 5% at every level, expected exit 6.2% vs 7.0% realized) and moves net NPV -$645M against -$1,186M for L alone.
- [FINDING] M (cap-hit-informed anchor) beats every stats-only rule by ~2.5 points; circular, so a ceiling, not a candidate. Top 50: -0.7% overall, -3.4% for 3+. S0 over-predicts exits with the production hazard. C under-corrects the 1-2 tier.
- [FINDING] Market line: error is not tilted by level (the over-valuation of stars is all projection). Admissible gains: one-season flag -5.3%, free two-season weights -6.3%, +age -7.2%, +games share -16.8%. Games carry their own price (~$2.1M full vs half season at the same WAR) and take the price per win from $2.03M to ~$0.8M. Contract length -30.6% but inadmissible (prices the contract off its own term); a back-test mispricing candidate.
- [FINDING] Games-aware line in the chain: relative error 56.5% -> 49.8% of realized value overall, 47% -> 40% for 3+, on its own currency; runs ~6% low; changes the D6-D9 definition of value. Design decision.
