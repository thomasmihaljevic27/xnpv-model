  the contract's PuckPedia expiry is "UFA no QO", a label that records the club's decision at the contract's end. A

  with that label (Hartman 2019-02-25, Donato, Duclair, Guhle). Not measured; the back-test's forecast side carries it.

  rule's cost, which runs 2-3 times below actual pay for first-rounders, in the forecast and the outcome alike, so pick
  and prospect surplus is high against players' on both sides. The club taking the futures shows a gain under that
  construction; actual pay for draftees (the option not chosen) is the sensitivity that would test it.

  LTIR (STANDING_FLAGS "LTIR as a strategic asset").
