## The age form decided (2026-10-24)

- Explained to Thomas: current age on September 15 of D+n = 18 + (n - 1) + years past first eligibility; within one
  season the age term ranks prospects by current age; years past first eligibility barely changes for a player (by
  0.002 at most) and is nearly uncorrelated with seasons since the draft (0.005, against 0.861 for current age); the
  variance inflation factors are 7 to 9 for production and its square, 2.4 to 2.9 for production x age. A step back in
  production lowers production itself (50/30/20); getting older is carried by the later season's lower coefficients.
  Corrected in chat: the discounted form's gain over the adopted model is not statistically significant (an earlier
  reply read as if it were).
- Decided (Thomas): "for now I will say to keep the model as is". Production x age stays; nothing downstream reruns.
