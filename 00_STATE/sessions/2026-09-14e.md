  norm. **Production exposure, not yet acted on:** the chain reads the WAR total so its
  levels are unaffected, but `component_persistence_test.py`'s covariance shares are computed
  over pairs including 2023-24 onward and normalised to sum to one, so they carry the
  residual inside them. Effect is small and has not been quantified.

- [FINDING] **The EP birthdates do not unblock the aging work, and the numbers say why.**
