## Three explanations corrected against the code

Read aging_curve.py and skater_forward_projection.py before building. Three
statements made during the session do not match the code, and the workbook and
explainer carry the corrected versions:
- The yardstick is not built per age. AgingModel._build_bank samples up to
  1,200 profiles per position from ALL ages (seed 0), measures within-position
  pairwise distances, pools forwards and defence, and takes one median.
- 0.61 is not the median distance. It is the weight at one yardstick,
  exp(-1/2) = 0.607 (two yardsticks: 0.135).
- "Tries the season before" is the target's base-age fallback (D21: age-1,
  then age-2), not a weighted average of a comparable's seasons.
Also made explicit: comparable weights are used twice (the 45% pull toward
"players like him" and each yearly step), both blended with the league
position-age average at SHRINK_K = 10 units; style measures are shares of
gross style WAR with Pens dropped; standardisation is over all forward
profiles, not one age.
