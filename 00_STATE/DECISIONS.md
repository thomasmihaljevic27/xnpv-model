Also found: **PuckPedia's `overall_position` is back-filled for future picks** — a 2020 third traded in June 2019 carries its realized slot 70, a number nobody knew on the day. It must never be read for an ex-ante price.

**D28 (locked 2026-09-13, Thomas) — a signed extension is part of the asset from its signing date.**

- **Rule.** A valuation of season t0 as of date d covers the contract being played in t0 plus the contract that starts the season after it ends, if that contract was signed on or before d, repeated down the chain. Extension seasons are priced as contract seasons (survival × projected value − real cap hit, discounted). The RFA terminal value attaches to the end of the chain, so a player extended into his UFA years carries none. Signing dates come from the PuckPedia contract export by `contract_id`; a contract with no signing date is never added. d defaults to July 1 of t0, the date the page comes into force, and may be any date through June 30 of t0+1; the engine refuses a date outside that window. Implemented once, in `skater_forward_projection.contract_chain()` / `check_as_of()`, and used by the skater projection, the terminal value and the goalie branch.
- **What it replaces.** An undocumented code-level exclusion: `project_contract()` kept only the active contract, a look-ahead guard from before signing dates were joined. That priced a counterfactual for any player already extended. Lane Hutson after 2025-10-13 carried $17.65M of RFA control value at qualifying-offer cost for years he had signed at $8.85M; under D28 the same page as of that date is −$14.19M.
- **Measured.** Back-test spine (each contract from its first 2018-2025 season): 0 of 2,981 contracts move. Panel pages valued as of July 1: 18 of 6,939 move (16 down, 2 up, net −$297.9M), every one carrying a newly included extension and none without. Few extensions are signed by July 1 of the season they follow, because the CBA only allows extending in a contract's final year, so most enter the dashboard as in-season variants dated to the signing.
- **Identification.** Gated strictly on the information date; no realized outcome enters. **Not gated:** the active contract's own signing date, so a deal signed in August for season t0 is on the t0 page from July 1 (flagged in STANDING_FLAGS).
